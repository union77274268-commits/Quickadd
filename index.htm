Questions

Good to know.

Can furniture be made to my size?

Yes. Sofas, beds and other pieces can be made to your size, design and finish. Use the studio above, visit the showroom, or send an enquiry with your room measurements.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No. The showroom is open every day from 10 AM to 8 PM. For a longer design conversation, send an enquiry and the team will set aside time for you.

How does ordering from this site work?

Add pieces to your cart, then send the order request to the showroom on WhatsApp. The team confirms availability, final price and delivery with you before anything is charged.

What about delivery and care?

Delivery time and care depend on the piece and the finish you choose. Ask the team when you confirm your order and they will walk you through both.

Where exactly is the showroom?

Shop No. 211, Takiya ki Chowki, opposite the Power House and near Ravan Gate on Kalwar Road, Jhotwara, Jaipur. Look for the lit Beyond Living sign.
